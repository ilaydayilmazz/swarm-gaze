Noticing

The machine turns the image into a map of what stands out: strong edges and saturated colour. It is a crude kind of attention, with no feeling behind it.

Lingering

A swarm of flies moves over that map using Dispersive Flies Optimisation. Flies stay longer where the image stands out, then grow bored and move on. Where they linger is what the machine has noticed.

Pigment

Each fly releases particles carrying the colour it is looking at. The particles drift on a slow flow field and fade, so the picture becomes a moving record of the swarm's gaze.

A simplified study inspired by Dispersive Flies Optimisation (al-Rifaie, 2014) and swarmic paintings with colour attention (al-Rifaie & Bishop, 2013), with a visual language that nods to the data-pigment works of Refik Anadol.

Prototype by İlayda Yılmaz with an AI coding assistant, MSc Data Science, University of Greenwich, October 2026.
